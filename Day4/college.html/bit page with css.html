Who We Are

Bhilai Institute of Technology, Durg is a reputed technical institute located in Durg, Chhattisgarh. The institute focuses on providing quality education and developing skilled engineering professionals.


Students are encouraged to participate in technical, cultural, sports and extracurricular activities for their overall development.

Our Vision

To develop competent professionals with technical knowledge, creativity, innovation and ethical values who can contribute to society.
